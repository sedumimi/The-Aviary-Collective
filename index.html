Voices shaping contemporary Kenyan art

Residence at Avian Court

Selected works by Aura Gallery artists are displayed throughout Avian Court Hotel, including hallways and guest rooms. The collection offers hotel guests the opportunity to experience Kenyan art in a deeply local and lived setting, while also introducing local and international visitors to the energy and character of contemporary Kenyan practice.

For artists, this setting creates visibility in a meaningful cultural space. For collectors, it offers the chance to discover original work in an authentic hospitality environment and to connect with the stories behind each piece. Aura Gallery remains the focus: a platform for artists, a home for Kenyan creativity, and a place where art can be encountered, collected, and appreciated.

About Aura Gallery

Art, community, and place

Aura Gallery was born from a passion for art, community, and the remarkable beauty of the place we call home.

Our vision is to share the spirit of Naivasha, its landscapes, its people, and its stories, while celebrating the richness and diversity of Kenya through art. Displayed throughout the hallways and guest rooms of Avian Court Hotel, each painting offers our guests a deeper connection to the destination and the creative voices that bring it to life.

The gallery provides talented local artists with a platform to present their work to Avian Court’s broad and diverse clientele. By connecting these artists with guests from Kenya and around the world, we hope to expand their visibility, create meaningful opportunities, and ensure that local creativity receives the recognition it deserves.

Every painting is available for purchase, allowing our guests to take home more than a beautiful work of art—they carry with them a piece of Naivasha, a story of Kenya, and a lasting connection to the artist who created it.

Aura Gallery is where hospitality, culture, and creativity come together—creating space for local art to be seen, appreciated, and shared with the world.

To be considered for the Aura Gallery collection, please contact our team with a brief introduction, examples of your artwork, pricing, and the inspiration behind your work.

Let us work together to give Kenyan artists a wider platform and share the beauty, stories, and creative spirit of Kenya with the world.

Calling Artists Across Kenya

Open call for Kenyan artists

Are you a Kenyan artist with a story to share through your work?

Aura Gallery at Avian Court Hotel welcomes emerging and established artists from across Kenya whose work reflects the country’s landscapes, people, cultures, experiences, and creative spirit.

We are passionate about providing local artists with a platform to showcase their work to Avian Court’s diverse local and international guests. Selected artwork is displayed throughout the hotel’s hallways and guest rooms, creating opportunities for artists to gain visibility, connect with potential collectors, and offer their work for purchase.

We are looking for original, thoughtfully created pieces that celebrate Kenya’s identity in all its richness and diversity. Whether your inspiration comes from a particular community, landscape, tradition, memory, or contemporary experience, we would love to discover the story behind your art.

How to Buy

Discover and collect original art

Explore original paintings inspired by Kenya, African heritage, nature, community, culture and contemporary creativity.

Step 1 — Discover

Browse the collection and use the Gallery page's filters to narrow works by price range and size.

Step 2 — Explore the Artwork

Review photographs, artist, dimensions, price and other available information. Because every original artwork is unique, take time to review the details before purchasing.

Step 3 — Ask Questions

Ask about dimensions, framing, materials, availability, viewing, delivery, artwork details or commission possibilities.

Step 4 — Place Your Order

Add the artwork to your cart and follow the checkout process. Provide accurate contact and delivery information.

Step 5 — Payment

Complete payment using the options provided during checkout. Do not send payment to unofficial numbers or accounts.

Step 6 — Confirmation

Once payment and order details are verified, you will receive an order confirmation.

Step 7 — Preparation & Delivery

The artwork will be prepared according to its size, material, framing and destination, with reasonable care taken for transportation.

Shipping & Returns

Delivering art with care, handled fairly

Original artwork requires careful handling. We prepare purchased artworks with attention to packaging, protection and destination requirements.

Delivery Within Kenya

We deliver artworks from Naivasha to destinations within Kenya. Delivery times and costs depend on destination, artwork dimensions and weight, framing, packaging requirements, transport method and accessibility.

International Delivery & Customs

International delivery may be available for selected artworks; contact us before purchasing to confirm availability and estimated costs. International orders may be subject to customs duties, taxes or import charges, which are the buyer's responsibility unless expressly stated otherwise.

Receiving Your Artwork

Inspect the package and artwork as soon as reasonably possible. If damaged, photograph the artwork and packaging before disposal and contact us immediately with your order number and a description of the damage.

Returns & Refunds

Returns and refunds are handled according to the circumstances of each order, and nothing in this policy limits consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under Kenyan law. If you receive damaged or incorrect artwork, contact us before altering, framing or cleaning it. Because original artworks are unique and may be prepared specifically for delivery, change-of-mind returns may be subject to specific conditions.

Where a refund is approved, it will normally use the original payment method where reasonably possible; processing time may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.
